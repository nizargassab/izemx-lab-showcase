# IZEMX Lab

A full-stack contribution to an AI SaaS platform during a final-year internship at IZEMX Lab. The work connected secure backend services, a React back office and the operational services needed to manage AI usage.

**My role:** Full-Stack Developer · Final-year internship · March–August 2026  
**Author:** [Nizar Gassab](https://github.com/nizargassab)

[View project](https://izemx.com/lancement-agents-ia-personnalises-izemx-consultation/) · [Portfolio case study](https://nizar-gassab-portfolio.vercel.app/projets/izemx-lab/) · [LinkedIn](https://www.linkedin.com/in/nizar-gassab-b872b5244/)

## What I worked on

### 1. Secure microservices

Developed Java/Spring Boot services protected by Keycloak behind an API Gateway.

### 2. Business back office

Built React/TypeScript interfaces covering seven business modules.

### 3. Usage and integrations

Automated AI usage and credit tracking with LiteLLM and integrated Stripe, AWS S3 and n8n in a Docker/Kubernetes environment with Grafana monitoring.
